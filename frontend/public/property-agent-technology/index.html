How Property Agents Can Use Digital Technology to Win More Listings

The best agents have always won on trust and presentation. Today, a large part of that presentation is digital — and vendors can tell the difference in the first meeting.

Presentation quality is now a competitive advantage — before, during and after the pitch.

An agent's job splits into two challenges: winning the listing, and then selling it. Digital technology has quietly become one of the strongest levers on both. A vendor deciding between two agents will often go with the one who can show, concretely, how their property will be presented to the market. And once instructed, the same tools help the agent attract and filter buyers so their time goes to the people most likely to transact.

It Starts at the Pitch

Winning an instruction is a sales pitch, and the most persuasive pitch shows rather than tells. An agent who arrives with an example of a professional 360° virtual tour or Digital Twin is making a clear promise: this is how your property will be marketed. That's a far stronger position than a verbal description of "great photos and portal listings".

Vendors don't choose the agent with the best words. They choose the one who shows the best plan.

360° Virtual Tours

A 360° virtual tour lets prospective buyers walk through a listing at any hour, from anywhere. For the agent, that means a listing that markets itself around the clock and reaches buyers who can't attend in person. It also signals quality — a tour immediately positions the listing above others that offer only photos.

Digital Twins and Remote Viewing

For higher-value or harder-to-access properties, a Digital Twin adds a measurable, dimensionally accurate walkthrough. Buyers can explore layout and scale in detail and even reference measurements, which is particularly valuable for out-of-state and overseas buyers doing serious due diligence before travelling.

Aerial Photography

Location sells, and aerial and drone imagery communicates it instantly — the neighbourhood, connectivity, outlook and proximity to amenities. For landed homes, larger plots and developments, aerial context often does more to move a buyer than another interior shot.

Virtual Staging

Empty listings are the hardest to sell because buyers can't picture living in them. Virtual staging digitally furnishes a vacant unit so it feels like a home, helping buyers connect emotionally — without the cost and logistics of physically staging the space.

Property Websites and Microsites

A dedicated listing microsite gives a premium property a home of its own — tour, gallery, floor plans, location and enquiry form in one branded place the agent can share as a single link. It looks more professional than a portal listing and keeps the agent's brand front and centre.

Social Media Content

Immersive assets are also social assets. Clips from a virtual tour, aerial fly-throughs and staged reveals are exactly the kind of content that performs on social platforms — extending a listing's reach far beyond the portals and building the agent's personal brand at the same time.

Qualifying Buyers and Engagement

Perhaps the biggest practical win is time. When buyers can explore a property thoroughly online first, the ones who then request a viewing are already serious. That means fewer wasted appointments, richer conversations, and an agent whose hours go to genuine, well-informed prospects. Better digital property marketing doesn't just generate more leads — it generates better ones.

Bottom line

Technology doesn't replace the agent. It makes a good agent look — and perform — like the obvious choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does technology help property agents win listings?

Showing a vendor a professional 360° virtual tour or Digital Twin in the pitch demonstrates how their property will be marketed. A stronger marketing proposal is often what wins the instruction over a competing agent.

Do virtual tours help sell property faster?

Virtual tours let buyers explore remotely and self-qualify, so the enquiries an agent receives tend to be more serious and better informed, reducing wasted viewings.

What technology should a property agent invest in first?

A quality 360° virtual tour is usually the highest-impact starting point, followed by aerial photography for context and virtual staging for empty units.
