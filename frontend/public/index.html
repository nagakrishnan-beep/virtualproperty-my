Spatial Technology, Explained

What is a Digital Twin for property? A property Digital Twin is an accurate, interactive 3D replica of a real space that lets buyers, tenants and teams explore a property online from any device.

What is a 360° virtual tour? A 360° virtual tour connects panoramic images into a navigable walkthrough so viewers can experience a property remotely before visiting.
